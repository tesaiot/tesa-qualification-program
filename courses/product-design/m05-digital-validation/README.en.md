
# Module 5 — Scenario and Digital Validation

*Scenario and Digital Validation* · [Product Industrial Design (Blender & Digital Twin)](../README.md) course

## Objectives

Find design bugs on screen before wasting plastic: run usage scenarios on the Twin, pair firmware/edge AI data with the model, and produce a fix list before printing a prototype.

## Lessons

| # | Lesson | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Digital validation before building a prototype](l01-scenario-digital-validation/README.md) | Designing a test scenario before clicking anything, observing and adjusting the design, tying the model to firmware/edge AI data with evidence, and a pre-print checklist summary |
| 2 | [Lab: usage scenarios and a pre-prototype checklist](l02-lab/README.md) | Writing the scenario before clicking, running it and noting problems, binding data or recording the gap, then filling in the pre-prototype checklist |

Approximate time per the original: about 3 hours (lessons) + a 2.5–3 hour lab.

Accompanying sheets and templates (in the `resources/` folder of lesson 1):

- [pre-prototype-checklist.md](l01-scenario-digital-validation/resources/pre-prototype-checklist.md)

## Checkpoint

Before moving to the next module, check that you can do the following:

- [ ] At least two usage scenarios with screenshots
- [ ] At least three problems or watch-points identified
- [ ] Evidence of a data binding, or a written explanation of the gap
- [ ] A pre-prototype checklist with the top 3 fixes
