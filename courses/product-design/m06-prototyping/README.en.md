
# Module 6 — Prototyping and the Final Project

*Prototyping and Final Project* · [Product Industrial Design (Blender & Digital Twin)](../README.md) course

## Objectives

Close the loop from concept to something you can hold: prepare an STL, check fitment against a real board, test alongside firmware or the Twin, and deliver a Design Report.

## Lessons

| # | Lesson | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Production files, fitment prototyping, and the Design Report](l01-prototyping-final-project/README.md) | Checking the mesh and exporting STL, alternatives when you cannot print yet, checking fitment against a real board, testing alongside firmware, and writing a Design Report |
| 2 | [Capstone lab: a prototype package and Design Report](l02-lab/README.md) | Applying fixes from Module 5's results, checking the mesh, exporting STL, printing or making a print plan, checking fitment, testing alongside firmware/Twin, and submitting the Design Report |

Approximate time per the original: about 3 hours (lessons) + a ~3-hour lab + separate time for printing/assembling the prototype.

Accompanying sheets and templates (in the `resources/` folder of lesson 1):

- [design-report-template.md](l01-prototyping-final-project/resources/design-report-template.md)

## Checkpoint

Before moving to the next module, check that you can do the following:

- [ ] A `.blend` + `.glb` + at least one STL file
- [ ] A genuinely filled-in fitment table, or a print plan with measurement evidence
- [ ] A record of testing alongside firmware / the Twin / a web-app
- [ ] A Design Report covering every section, with ≥ 3 suggestions for the next round
