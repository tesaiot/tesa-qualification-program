
# Module 2 — Modeling, Materials, and Render

*Modeling, Materials, and Render* · [Product Industrial Design (Blender & Digital Twin)](../README.md) course

## Objectives

Develop the rough block from Module 1 into production-ready detail without breaking the locked-in dimensions, add PBR materials, set up lighting, and render a presentation image.

## Lessons

| # | Lesson | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Enclosure detail, PBR materials, and a presentation image](l01-modeling-materials-render/README.md) | Apply Scale, Solidify, Boolean, Bevel and splitting the lid/base, Principled BSDF materials, three-point lighting, camera, and rendering |
| 2 | [Lab: refine the model, add PBR materials, and render](l02-lab/README.md) | Lock the scale, add wall thickness, cut at least two openings, split the lid/base, add two PBR materials, then render |

Approximate time per the original: about 4 hours (lessons) + a 3.5–4 hour lab.

Accompanying sheets and templates (in the `resources/` folder of lesson 1):

- [material-lighting-sheet.md](l01-modeling-materials-render/resources/material-lighting-sheet.md)

## Checkpoint

Before moving to the next module, check that you can do the following:

- [ ] ≥ 2 openings cut, and the lid/base split into separate objects
- [ ] The PCB still fits, checked in Wireframe view
- [ ] ≥ 2 PBR materials, and ≥ 1 rendered PNG image
- [ ] material-lighting-sheet.md filled in completely
