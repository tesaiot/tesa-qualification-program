
# Module 3 — Motion and Interaction

*Motion and Interaction* · [Product Industrial Design (Blender & Digital Twin)](../README.md) course

## Objectives

Use animation as a tool to check the mechanism: set up a hinge, build named open–close clips, and catch part collisions before printing for real.

## Lessons

| # | Lesson | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Open–close animation and collision checking](l01-motion-and-interaction/README.md) | Preparing the lid's pivot, keyframing open–close, naming the Action, checking for collisions during playback, and preparing clips for the Twin |
| 2 | [Lab: lid animation and a short motion sequence](l02-lab/README.md) | Setting up the hinge, keyframing the `lid_open` clip, building a close clip, checking for collisions, and recording the clip list |

Approximate time per the original: about 3 hours (lessons) + a 2.5–3 hour lab.

Accompanying sheets and templates (in the `resources/` folder of lesson 1):

- [animation-clip-list.md](l01-motion-and-interaction/resources/animation-clip-list.md)

## Checkpoint

Before moving to the next module, check that you can do the following:

- [ ] The lid's origin sits at the hinge
- [ ] Open/close clips named with short English names, such as `lid_open`
- [ ] Collision-check results recorded
- [ ] animation-clip-list.md filled in, with image or clip evidence
