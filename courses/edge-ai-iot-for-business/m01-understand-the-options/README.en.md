
# Module 1 · Understand the options

**Module goal** Know what edge AI and IoT can genuinely help with, decide whether to think on the device, on the cloud, or a mix of both, and see all three cost buckets clearly.

About 90 minutes in total. No code, no board needed.

| Lesson | Topic | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [biz.m01.l01](l01-what-it-can-do/README.md) | What edge AI and IoT can and cannot do | 30 min |
| [biz.m01.l02](l02-edge-cloud-hybrid/README.md) | Edge, cloud or hybrid | 30 min |
| [biz.m01.l03](l03-cost-bom-team/README.md) | Cost, BOM, time and team | 30 min |

## End-of-module checkpoint

- [ ] Your problem statement covers all three parts (what to measure, what to decide, who acts on it next) and you can say which step of the pre-AI ladder it stops at
- [ ] You have chosen edge, cloud or hybrid for your problem, with a reason across at least two dimensions
- [ ] You have a list of the three cost buckets, and a list of questions to ask a vendor or contractor

These three things are the raw material for the decision canvas in the next module. Keep them safe.
