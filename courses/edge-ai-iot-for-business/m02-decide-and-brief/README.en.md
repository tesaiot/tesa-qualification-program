
# Module 2 · Decide and brief

**Module goal** See the risks and rules you must follow, choose between building it yourself, buying it, or finding a partner, then bring everything together into a one-page decision canvas.

About 90 minutes in total.

| Lesson | Topic | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [biz.m02.l01](l01-risk-and-compliance/README.md) | Risk and compliance | 30 min |
| [biz.m02.l02](l02-build-buy-partner/README.md) | Build, buy or partner | 30 min |
| [biz.m02.l03](l03-developer-brief/README.md) | Write a brief for a developer: a one-page decision canvas | 30 min |

## End-of-module checkpoint (the course's final deliverable)

- [ ] A risk register with at least four rows, covering cybersecurity, personal data, standards and the supply chain
- [ ] A weighted build/buy/partner table, with the option you chose
- [ ] A [decision canvas](l03-developer-brief/resources/decision-canvas.md) for your project, with all 15 boxes filled in
