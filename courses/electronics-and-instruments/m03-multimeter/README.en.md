# Module 3 · The multimeter

**Module goal:** measure voltage, continuity and current safely and correctly.

Status **alpha** (in trial use) · about 130 minutes

**You need:** a multimeter with its manual, a breadboard, an LED, a resistor, and a board

| Lesson | Topic | Time |

## Module checkpoint

- [ ] can measure the 3.3 V rail and the voltage from a knob on the board, and compare it against what the ADC reads
- [ ] can measure one LED's current in series, and always move the red lead back to the VΩ jack afterwards
- [ ] can find a short circuit on a powered-off board using continuity mode
