LicenseRef-PublicDomain

Works in the public domain, for example works of the United States federal government
(such as NOAA, DARPA or NHTSA images) or works whose copyright term has expired.

No copyright restrictions apply to these works. The source and creator are credited in CREDITS.md
and in the course's credits.yaml as a courtesy and so that readers can check the status themselves.
Public-domain status can differ between jurisdictions; check it before reusing a work outside
Thailand or the United States.
